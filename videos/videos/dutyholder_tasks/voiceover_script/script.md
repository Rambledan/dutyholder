# Voiceover script: Your Part 2A duties in Dutyholder

## dh-intro

You've been named as a dutyholder on a building project. Here's what happens next, and how to get it done in about fifteen minutes.

## dh-email

It starts with an email from whoever appointed you. It explains why: under Part 2A, everyone who designs or builds must acknowledge their duties and show they're competent. Click Accept invite.

## dh-signup

Your email is already filled in. Add your name and a password to create your account.

## dh-two-factor

Then turn on two-factor login with an authenticator app on your phone. It keeps your records secure.

## dh-tasks

You land on your tasks for the project. Each one is a legal duty, and you can see your progress as you go. Click Start.

## dh-acknowledge

Most tasks are acknowledgements. Read the duty in plain English, tick to confirm, and type your name to sign. Dutyholder records when you signed, and the exact wording you agreed to.

## dh-competence

The competence statement takes longest. It belongs to you, not the project. Describe your skills, knowledge and experience, and how you'd handle work that wouldn't comply. Upload your evidence: qualifications, memberships and insurance. Then submit it for review.

## dh-report-change

You'll also agree to tell whoever appointed you if you ever stop being competent for the work. Tick, sign, done.

## dh-done

And that's it, you're done for now. Whoever appointed you reviews your statement, and you'll hear from them if anything else is needed.

## dh-passport

Your details and competence statement are saved to your account. Next time someone invites you, reuse them in one click.

## dh-outro

Your duties, recorded once and reused on every project. That's Dutyholder.
