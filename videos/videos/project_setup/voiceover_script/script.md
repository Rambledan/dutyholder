# Voiceover script: Setting up a project in Dutyholder

## ps-intro

Dutyholder helps you manage your Part 2A dutyholder duties. Here's how a principal designer sets up a new project.

## ps-signup

You start from an invite. Create your account with your name and a strong password.

## ps-two-factor

Then turn on two-factor login with an authenticator app, and save your recovery codes somewhere safe.

## ps-new-project

From your projects page, click New project. Give it a name, a site address and a short description.

## ps-help-marks

Not sure what a term means? Hover over any question mark for a plain-English explanation.

## ps-scoping

A few scoping questions follow: is it higher-risk, is the client domestic, and how many contractors will there be? Your answers decide which duties apply to whom.

## ps-project-created

Create the project, and the set-up is summarised at the top.

## ps-add-team

Next, add your team under their statutory roles: the client, designers and their disciplines, and the principal contractor. A firm can hold an appointment too.

## ps-invite

Click Invite, and each person gets an email with a secure link. You can see who has joined and who hasn't.

## ps-checklists

Dutyholder builds everyone's checklist for you: acknowledgements, competence statements and evidence, all tied to the regulations.

## ps-dashboard

The dashboard shows each person's progress in red, amber and green. Reminders go out automatically, so you don't have to chase.

## ps-outro

That's your project set up, in a few minutes. Dutyholder.
